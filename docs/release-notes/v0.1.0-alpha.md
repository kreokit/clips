**Free, offline short clips from your long videos.** This is the first public alpha of KreoKit Clips: drop in a podcast, interview, talk or livestream, and Clips transcribes it, finds the best moments, reframes them to vertical 9:16 following the speaker, adds styled captions and exports them all, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Everything runs on your own computer. No account, no upload, no watermark.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Expect rough edges. Please report problems at https://github.com/kreokit/clips/issues (or ⋯ → Report a problem in the app).

## What it does

- **Transcribe long videos offline** with Whisper (Base: fast, Small: balanced, Large: most accurate), in parts, with progress and an estimated time left. Cancel any time and resume where you stopped.
- **Find the best moments:** clips of whole sentences, scored for a strong hook, a clean start and ending, energy and length, with "Why this clip" showing the reasons. Presets for **TikTok Rewards (61–90 s)**, **Shorts / Reels (20–60 s)** or your own lengths.
- **Review and adjust:** keep or discard clips, trim them on a timeline with the waveform and the words (handles snap to sentence and word edges), or make your own from selected text.
- **Reframe to 9:16:** follows the speaker's face, cutting (never panning) when the speaker or camera changes; or Center, Fit + blur (the whole picture over a blurred copy), or Manual (drag the crop).
- **Captions:** styles and positions from KreoKit Captions, drawn on the 9:16 preview, with the social apps' safe area, a warning when captions cover a face, and word fixes by double-click.
- **Export all:** 1080×1920 MP4 (H.264, AAC) with captions burned in, optional SRT files, numbered names (`video-clip-01.mp4`), checked after writing (TikTok Rewards clips are verified to last over 60.0 s), a free-space check, and nothing overwritten without asking.

**Requirements:** macOS 12 Monterey or later, Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or 11, or Linux with glibc 2.35+ (e.g. Ubuntu 22.04+). On Intel / AMD computers the processor needs AVX2, FMA and F16C (most Intel CPUs since 2013 and AMD CPUs since 2015); the app tells you if yours doesn't.

**AI models:** the first transcription downloads the Whisper model you choose (Base 148 MB, Small 488 MB, Large 1.6 GB; Small is the default), and the first reframe downloads the face model (2 MB). If KreoKit Captions has already downloaded the same Whisper model, Clips uses that copy. These one-time downloads are the app's only internet use.

## Installing an unsigned app

The alpha isn't signed with paid developer certificates, so your system will warn you the first time.

**macOS**
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **KreoKit Clips** to Applications.
2. Open it. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer", open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** (on macOS 12: **System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General**), and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
3. If macOS instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", that is the same missing signature, not real damage. Run this once in Terminal, then open the app again:
   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/KreoKit Clips.app"
   ```

**Windows**
1. Run the `…-setup.exe` installer.
2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Linux**
- AppImage: `chmod +x KreoKit.Clips_*.AppImage`, then run it.
- Debian / Ubuntu: `sudo apt install ./KreoKit.Clips_*_amd64.deb`.

## Known limitations

- Everything runs on the CPU (no GPU acceleration yet): transcribing an hour of video takes a while on older computers. The computer is kept awake while a job runs (macOS and Windows).
- The moment finder is tuned for English; for other languages the hook isn't judged.
- The speaker is the biggest, most central face; Clips doesn't yet tell who is talking from mouth movement. No zoom or split screen for two speakers yet.
- One caption style per project.
- A source whose sound is out of sync with its picture can't be corrected in the app yet.
- Older SD videos (BT.601 colour) come out with slightly shifted colours.
